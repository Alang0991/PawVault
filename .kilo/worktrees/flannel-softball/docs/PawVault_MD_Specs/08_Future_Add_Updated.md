**# PawVault — Future Additions**

\> FUTURE BACKLOG ONLY.

\>

\> Do not build everything here now.

\>

\> Prioritize based on real user/creator demand, platform stability, moderation capacity, legal requirements, and whether the feature genuinely improves PawVault.

**---**

**## Principle**

Do not turn PawVault into a copy of another marketplace.

Borrow useful ideas from other platforms when appropriate, but build PawVault's own identity, systems, community, and creator experience.

Every future feature should answer at least one of these:

\- Does this help creators earn?

\- Does this help customers discover something better?

\- Does this improve trust?

\- Does this improve the buying experience?

\- Does this make PawVault meaningfully better for the creator community?

\- Does this create a useful new part of the ecosystem?

Do not build features purely because another marketplace has them.

# CREATOR OWNERSHIP & PAWVAULT AUTHORITY

> CRITICAL: PawVault is the marketplace platform, not the owner of creator content.

Creators own and control their own stores, products, files, artwork, commission work, pricing, descriptions, and creator content, subject to the platform Terms, licenses, moderation policies, and applicable law.

Founder, Admin, and Moderator access must never be treated as automatic ownership of creator content.

## PawVault MAY

- Review reported content
- Review products for marketplace-rule compliance
- Request changes from a creator
- Hide a product from public discovery
- Suspend a product
- Suspend a creator store
- Suspend a creator account
- Ban a creator/store where policy permits
- Handle reports
- Handle copyright/DMCA processes
- Handle fraud and safety investigations
- Restrict marketplace access
- Preserve evidence
- Handle appeals
- Enforce platform-wide rules

Every enforcement action must be auditable.

## PawVault MUST NOT casually control

Normal Founder/Admin/Moderator tooling must NOT provide unrestricted controls to:

- Change another creator's product price
- Change another creator's files
- Edit another creator's product description
- Change another creator's license
- Make another creator's paid product free
- Give away another creator's paid product
- Transfer another creator's store
- Transfer product ownership
- Change creator payout settings
- Change commission pricing
- Publish private creator content
- Claim creator content as PawVault content
- Permanently delete creator-owned content as a normal moderation action

There must never be a generic `manage_everything` permission that bypasses creator ownership boundaries.

## Free Product Protection

A Founder or Admin must NOT have a normal `Make Free` action on another creator's paid product.

Creators control their own product pricing.

If PawVault later offers platform-wide promotions, discounts, coupons, bundles, or sponsored events, those systems must be explicitly designed around creator participation and the applicable platform terms.

PawVault must never silently change a creator's price.

## Moderation Is Not Ownership

A moderation action is not an ownership transfer.

A product can be:

- Reported
- Reviewed
- Hidden
- Suspended
- Appealed
- Restored

without PawVault becoming its owner.

## Store Suspension

When a store is suspended:

- Public discovery can be disabled
- New purchases can be restricted according to policy
- The creator is notified
- The reason is recorded
- The action is audited
- Appeals can be supported
- Creator ownership remains separate from platform moderation

## Account Ban

A ban is a platform-access action.

It does NOT mean:

> PawVault now owns everything this creator made.

Account enforcement and content ownership must remain separate concepts.

# FINAL OWNERSHIP PRINCIPLE

> **PawVault owns the platform. Creators own their creations.**

PawVault can moderate access to the marketplace.

PawVault can suspend or ban stores.

PawVault can protect customers and the community.

PawVault can enforce its rules.

But platform authority must never be confused with ownership of creator content.

If a creator is suspended or banned, that does not automatically make their products PawVault property.

If a product is hidden, that does not mean PawVault owns it.

If a customer needs a free support entitlement, do not make the creator's product free.

If a creator violates the rules, enforce the rules through moderation.

**Moderate the marketplace. Do not take ownership of the creators.**
