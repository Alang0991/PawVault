# PawVault — Founder, Staff & Moderation

## Roles

Maintain:
- FOUNDER
- ADMIN
- MODERATOR
- CREATOR
- USER

All authorization is server-side.

## Founder protection

Founder cannot be demoted, suspended, banned, deleted or have permissions removed by lower roles.

## Founder Control Center

Include real management for:
- Overview
- Users
- Creators
- Products
- Orders
- Reviews
- Reports
- Feedback
- Moderation
- Categories
- Featured
- Discounts
- Creator Applications
- Staff
- Permissions
- Announcements
- Roadmap
- Changelog
- Help Center
- Settings
- Audit Logs

## Dashboard

Real metrics only:
- users
- creators
- products
- orders
- revenue
- reports
- applications
- moderation queue
- support tickets
- feedback

## User management

Support search, profile, status, roles, creator state, reports, moderation history, suspend/unsuspend and ban/unban.

Dangerous actions require confirmation.

## Creator management

Support application review, approval, rejection, suspension, store review, product review, internal notes and history.

## Product moderation

Staff can review, approve, reject, hide, restore and archive.

Every decision records actor, target, reason and timestamp.

## Reports

Standard fields:
- reporter
- target
- target type
- reason
- description
- status
- assignee
- resolution
- timestamps

Statuses:
- New
- Under Review
- Action Required
- Resolved
- Dismissed

## Moderation queue

Centralize:
- user reports
- product reports
- review reports
- feedback reports
- creator reports
- support escalations

Support search, filters, assignment, priority and resolution.

## Staff management

Founder can add staff, change roles, assign permissions, remove staff and view staff activity.

Never expose staff passwords or secrets.

## Audit logs

Audit:
- role changes
- permission changes
- bans
- suspensions
- moderation
- report resolution
- refunds
- staff changes
- settings changes
- feedback moderation
- creator approvals

Never log passwords, tokens, cookies or secrets.

## Future moderation

Plan for:
- spam detection
- malware scanning
- copyright/DMCA workflows
- appeals
- moderation analytics
- repeat offender tracking
- escalation rules

Automation should assist staff, not silently punish users without an appropriate review path.
